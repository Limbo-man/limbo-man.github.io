The Roadmap

The 40-Day Curriculum

A structured, milestone-driven program. Each phase builds on the last. You won't just learn tools — you'll learn how to approach problems you've never seen before.

Days 1–10

Foundations & The Security Mindset

Before you can break things, you need to understand how they work — and how they break in the real world, not just in textbooks. This phase is about building the analytical framework that everything else sits on top of.

How networks actually behave vs. what textbooks say
TCP/IP, DNS, DHCP — practical, not theoretical
Thinking in attack surfaces and trust boundaries
Nmap: more than just scanning ports
Passive reconnaissance and OSINT fundamentals
Problem decomposition: breaking down unknowns
Days 11–20

Web Application Security

Web apps are where most junior pentesters start their careers. This phase covers the mechanics behind common vulnerabilities and, more importantly, how to find them manually — because automated scanners will only get you so far.

HTTP/S: request/response mechanics deep dive
OWASP Top 10 — hands-on, not just definitions
SQL Injection, XSS, IDOR: finding & exploiting
Burp Suite: from basics to advanced workflows
Authentication & session mechanisms
When scanners miss things: manual testing logic
Days 21–30

Practical Application, CTFs & Self-Learning

Cybersecurity evolves every week. The most valuable skill you can build is knowing how to learn on your own. This phase is about guided practice, CTF problem-solving, and building the habits that will sustain your career long after the 40 days are over.

Guided CTF walkthroughs with real methodology
Hack The Box & TryHackMe: how to actually use them
Building your own safe practice lab (VMs, containers)
Researching unfamiliar technologies efficiently
Documentation & note-taking habits for pentesters
When you're stuck: systematic troubleshooting
Days 31–40

Corporate Reality & Interview Preparation

Technical skills get you the interview. Professionalism gets you the job. This phase covers how security teams actually operate in companies, how to present your work, and how to walk into an interview with confidence.

Writing professional pentest reports
Risk rating & prioritizing findings for clients
How to behave in a corporate security engagement
Mock technical interviews with real questions
Resume & LinkedIn positioning for security roles
Common mistakes junior pentesters make on the job

Common Questions

FAQ

How long is the program?

40 days, structured across four phases. It's intensive by design — enough time to build real skills and habits, short enough to maintain momentum. Think of it as a focused sprint, not a marathon.

Do I need prior cybersecurity experience?

No. You need basic programming familiarity (any language) and comfort with a terminal. The program starts from foundational concepts and builds up. If you've already done some CTFs, great — but it's not required.

Is it remote or in-person?

Primarily remote, with flexible scheduling. We'll do live sessions, guided walkthroughs, and regular check-ins. The hands-on labs are all done on your own machine.

What if I fall behind?

The 40-day timeline is a structure, not a deadline. If you need more time on a phase, we'll adjust. The goal is understanding, not speed. I'd rather you properly learn something than rush through it.

Will I get a certificate?

The value of this program isn't a piece of paper — it's the skills, the mindset, and the portfolio of practical work you'll build. That said, I'll provide a completion reference you can use on your resume and LinkedIn.

What makes this different from a Udemy course?

Accountability, mentorship, and real-world context. Video courses can't answer your questions, can't review your work, and can't tell you whether you're approaching a problem the right way. I can, and I will.
