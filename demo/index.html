Common Questions

Before You Decide

Is 40 days really enough to make a difference?

40 days of focused, structured mentorship can build the foundation that most people spend months or more trying to develop on their own. The timeline is intentionally intensive — it's designed around how professionals actually upskill, not how long it takes to watch a video library.

Do I need prior security experience?

Basic programming familiarity and terminal comfort are the only prerequisites. The program is designed to meet you where you are and build upward. Many of the most effective security professionals started with less than you might think.

How is this different from online courses?

Courses teach content. Mentorship teaches capability. When you're stuck on a CTF at 11pm, a video can't ask you what you've tried. I can. The difference between knowing about something and being able to do it usually comes down to one thing: someone who's been there, guiding you through it.

What if I can't keep up with the pace?

The 40-day structure provides momentum, but it's not a rigid deadline. The goal is genuine understanding. If a phase needs more time, we adjust. The cohort size is intentionally small so I can work with your pace, not against it.

Will this guarantee me a job?

No program can guarantee employment — and anyone who tells you otherwise isn't being honest. What this does is significantly strengthen your positioning: practical skills, professional methodology, and the confidence that comes from actually knowing what you're talking about in an interview. That tends to make a material difference.

Is it remote?

Primarily remote, with flexible scheduling. All hands-on work is done on your own machine. Live sessions and check-ins are arranged around your availability.
